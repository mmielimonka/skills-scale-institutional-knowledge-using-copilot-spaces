# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers research user needs, design intuitive interfaces, and validate usability. They collaborate with Product Managers, Developers, and stakeholders to ensure solutions are user-centered and accessible.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Create wireframes, mockups, and interactive prototypes
- Define information architecture and user flows
- Collaborate with developers to ensure design feasibility and implementation quality
- Maintain design systems and UI component libraries
- Advocate for accessibility and inclusive design

### Goals
- Deliver user-friendly, accessible experiences
- Validate designs through research and testing
- Ensure consistency across product touchpoints

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- User research findings and personas
- Prototypes and design specifications
- Collaboration with developers on implementation details

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Align on user needs, feature priorities, and success metrics; validate designs against acceptance criteria
- **Developers**: Collaborate on design implementation, review UI components, provide design assets and specifications
- **Project Managers**: Participate in planning to estimate design effort, report progress on design deliverables
- **QA/Testing**: Partner on usability testing and acceptance testing
- **Business Analysts**: Review user flows and requirements to ensure designs address business needs
- **Stakeholders**: Present design concepts and gather feedback

---

## Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove impediments, and coach the team on agile practices. They enable the team to self-organize and continuously improve their delivery process.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers and impediments that slow the team
- Coach team members on agile principles and practices
- Shield the team from outside distractions and interruptions
- Track and communicate team metrics (velocity, burndown)
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement

### Goals
- Maximize team productivity and flow
- Build a high-performing, self-organizing team
- Enable predictable, sustainable delivery

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Retrospective facilitation and action tracking
- Impediment escalation to leadership
- Metrics dashboards and team health reports

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Coach on agile practices, remove blockers, facilitate collaboration
- **Product Managers**: Facilitate backlog refinement, help prioritize work, communicate team capacity
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate on dependencies and timelines, align on risk mitigation
- **QA/Testing**: Ensure quality practices are integrated into the sprint workflow
- **DevOps Engineers**: Collaborate on CI/CD improvements and deployment automation
- **Stakeholders**: Communicate team progress and impediments

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge business needs and technical solutions. They gather requirements, define processes, and ensure delivered solutions align with business objectives and stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business requirements
- Create process flows and business rule definitions
- Analyze data to inform decision-making
- Define acceptance criteria and validation scenarios
- Facilitate requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Validate that solutions meet business needs

### Goals
- Ensure clear, traceable requirements
- Maximize business value from technical solutions
- Reduce rework through thorough requirements analysis

### Typical Communication
- Requirements documents and user stories
- Process diagrams and workflow maps
- Data analysis and business case presentations
- Stakeholder interviews and workshops

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on feature definition, translate product vision into detailed requirements
- **Developers**: Clarify requirements, answer questions during implementation, validate solutions
- **Project Managers**: Support planning with effort estimation, identify dependencies and risks
- **UX Designers**: Review user flows, ensure designs meet business requirements
- **QA/Testing**: Define test scenarios based on requirements and acceptance criteria
- **Stakeholders**: Gather requirements, validate solutions, communicate impacts

---

## DevOps Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps Engineers build and maintain the infrastructure, tooling, and automation that enable reliable, efficient software delivery. They collaborate across teams to improve deployment pipelines, monitoring, and operational excellence.

### Responsibilities
- Design and maintain CI/CD pipelines
- Manage infrastructure as code and cloud resources
- Implement monitoring, logging, and alerting
- Automate deployment and operational tasks
- Ensure security best practices in infrastructure and pipelines
- Support incident response and root cause analysis

### Goals
- Enable fast, safe, automated deployments
- Maximize system reliability and observability
- Reduce manual operational toil

### Typical Communication
- Infrastructure and deployment documentation
- Incident postmortems and runbooks
- Pipeline and monitoring dashboards
- Security and compliance reports

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers**: Provide CI/CD tooling, collaborate on deployment strategies, support debugging production issues
- **Project Managers**: Report on infrastructure readiness, coordinate deployment windows, escalate operational risks
- **Product Managers**: Align on non-functional requirements (performance, reliability, scalability)
- **QA/Testing**: Integrate automated tests into pipelines, provide test environments
- **Scrum Master**: Participate in sprint planning for infrastructure work, report blockers
- **Stakeholders**: Communicate operational status, security posture, and infrastructure costs
